Questions, answered

What does Airbnb coaching include?

You work one-on-one with Kyle on your listing, pricing and guest experience, and get a clear list of what to change.

Can you set up the automation for me?

Yes. If you’d rather not do it yourself, we can set up full Airbnb and AI automation for your listing, including automatic guest messages and check-in details.

I’m setting up a new BnB. Can you help?

Yes. TurnKey BnBs helps with setup as well as consulting. Start with our free BnB Setup Checklist, then get in touch.

Do I need to be on the Gold Coast?

No. We’re Gold Coast based and work with hosts across Australia.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Results depend on your property, location and market, and on how the recommendations are applied. See our Disclaimer.
