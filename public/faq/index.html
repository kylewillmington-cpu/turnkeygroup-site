FAQs

Questions, answered

Everything people ask us about TurnKey Stays, TurnKey BnBs, TurnKey Access, TurnKey Pro and TurnKey Services.

All questions

The things people ask us most. Can’t see yours? Email kyle@turnkeybnbs.com.au.

What is TurnKey Group?

TurnKey Group is the collective name for five Gold Coast divisions: TurnKey Stays, TurnKey BnBs, TurnKey Access, TurnKey Pro and TurnKey Services. Each has its own website, and all are run by the same team at Netalyst Corporation Pty Ltd, trading as TurnKey BnBs.

How do I book a stay?

Head to turnkeystays.com.au, pick a Gold Coast or Brisbane property and your dates, and book online.

Can I bring my pet?

Most of our properties are pet friendly. Each listing shows its own pet rules, so check the listing before you book.

What does Airbnb coaching include?

You work one-on-one with Kyle on your listing, pricing and guest experience, and get a clear list of what to change. Find out more at turnkeybnbs.com.au.

Can you run the automation for me?

Yes. If you'd rather not do it yourself, we can set up full Airbnb automation for your listing. Get in touch and we'll talk through what suits your property.

How does the key safe wall work?

Rent a box on our wall at Shop 2, 14–16 Tedder Ave, Main Beach. It's open 6am to 10pm, and you can pick your box online at turnkeyaccess.com.au. Monthly and 12-month plans are available.

When can keys be collected from the wall?

Every day from 6am to 10pm. The wall is locked overnight.

Who can collect keys from my box?

Anyone you share your box's code with, such as guests, cleaners or tradespeople. We never publish box codes.

When does TurnKey Pro launch?

TurnKey Pro isn't available yet. Add your email on the TurnKey Pro page and we'll let you know when it launches.

What is TurnKey Services?

Our newest division, coming soon. TurnKey guests will be able to have the fridge stocked before they arrive and book extras before, during and after their stay. Get launch updates.

How do I get in touch?

Email kyle@turnkeybnbs.com.au or call 0436 322 508. We're available 7am to 7pm.
