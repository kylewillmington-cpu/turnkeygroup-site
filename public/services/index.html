FAQs

Questions, answered

Can't see yours? Email kyle@turnkeybnbs.com.au.

When does TurnKey Services launch?

Soon. Add your email above and we’ll send you one email when it launches.

Who can use TurnKey Services?

Guests staying in a TurnKey property. You’ll book extras for your specific stay, so everything arrives at the right place at the right time.

What services will there be?

We’re starting with a few, beginning with fridge stocking before you arrive, and adding more extras for during and after your stay over time.

How will I pay?

Online, when you book. Details will be on turnkeyservices.com.au at launch.
