Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026. This policy covers turnkeygroup.com.au, run by Netalyst Corporation Pty Ltd (ABN 69 688 734 652), trading as TurnKey BnBs, Gold Coast, Queensland (“we”, “us”). TurnKey Group is the collective name for our divisions: TurnKey Stays, TurnKey BnBs, TurnKey Access, TurnKey Pro and TurnKey Services. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

Only what we need, and only what you give us:

  • TurnKey Pro and TurnKey Services launch updates: your email address. Nothing else is required.
  • When you email or call us: your name, contact details and whatever you tell us about your enquiry.

We don’t ask for payment details, ID or your date of birth on this site.

What is collected automatically

Our website host keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) to keep the site running and secure. We don’t use analytics, advertising pixels, tracking cookies or session recording on this site. See our Cookie policy.

How we use it

To reply to you, provide the service you asked about, and send the launch update you signed up for. We don’t sell or rent your information, and we don’t add you to other mailing lists. Every email we send has a way to unsubscribe.

Services we use

  • Website hosting and forms: our host stores the site and receives the TurnKey Pro and TurnKey Services sign-up forms.
  • Email: our email provider handles messages you send us.
  • Google Fonts: your browser loads our font from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Images: the Gold Coast photo loads from Unsplash, and one photo loads from turnkeybnbs.com.au.
  • Free BnB Setup Checklist: delivered through our online store on Shopify, which has its own privacy and cookie policy.
  • Division websites: bookings and purchases happen on each division’s site, which has its own policy.

Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only as long as we need them. Your launch-update email is deleted when you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it.

Security

The site is served only over encrypted HTTPS, with security headers that block other sites from framing it or injecting scripts. We use reputable providers and limit access to your information to the people who need it.

Access, correction and complaints

To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to make a complaint, email kyle@turnkeybnbs.com.au or call 0436 322 508. If you’re not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
