FAQs

Questions, answered

Can't see yours? Email kyle@turnkeybnbs.com.au.

Where is the key safe wall?

Inside Tumble on Tedder, Shop 2, 14–16 Tedder Ave, Main Beach.

When can keys be collected?

Every day from 6am to 10pm. The wall is locked overnight, so no one can get to it outside those hours.

Who can collect keys from my box?

Anyone you share your box’s code with, such as guests, cleaners or tradespeople. We never publish box codes.

Can I change my code?

Please don’t change it yourself. Contact us and we’ll organise a code change.

What plans are there?

Monthly or 12-month plans. See current prices and pick your box at turnkeyaccess.com.au.
