One team, five divisions

TurnKey Group is the collective name for the TurnKey divisions: TurnKey Stays, TurnKey BnBs, TurnKey Access, TurnKey Pro and TurnKey Services. We’re Gold Coast based and provide services Australia-wide.

Guest experience, first

Everything we do comes back to the guest. The stay itself, the listing they book from, the key they collect and the small details they notice when they walk in. Each TurnKey division looks after one of those steps, and they all share the same people and the same standards.

We bring 20+ years of business experience, a Superhost-rated Airbnb listing and a home in Airbnb’s top 5%.
