Straight answers

What ATLAS can and can't do

No false promises. Here's where the limits are, so you know exactly what you're getting.

ATLAS can
  • Answer guest messages and calls, day and night
  • Vet bookings and flag risky ones
  • Set prices and work towards your revenue targets
  • Book cleaners, audits and tradespeople
  • Send weather warnings, bin reminders and lockout plans
  • Ring you, your guests or your trades when something's urgent
  • Run as many properties as you have
Where the limits are
  • Your software sets the limits. ATLAS can only do what your booking system and pricing tool allow it to connect to. We'll tell you before you sign up.
  • No hands-on work. It books the cleaner or the plumber, but someone still has to turn up.
  • Only as good as your property facts. That's why we log 100 points for every property.
  • Big calls stay with you. Refunds, damage claims and anything outside your rules come to you. In an emergency, guests are told to call 000.
  • Platform rules still apply. Airbnb and other sites have their own rules ATLAS has to follow.
  • Outages happen. If one of your providers goes down, ATLAS waits, retries and lets you know.
  • Calls at once depend on the voice plan. Very large portfolios get a bigger plan.

FAQs

Questions, answered

Can't see yours? Email kyle@turnkeybnbs.com.au.

When does ATLAS Core launch?

Soon. Apply above to join the waiting list and we'll contact you before launch.

Do I keep my own software?

Yes. Your channel manager, pricing tool and listings stay on your own accounts, in your name. You pay those providers directly.

Will ATLAS work with my systems?

Tell us what you use when you apply and we'll check. If ATLAS can't connect to your current setup, we'll recommend a setup it does connect to, and help you move.

Is my ATLAS shared with anyone?

No. Every client gets their own ATLAS, with its own logins, data and phone number.

Does ATLAS tell callers it's an AI?

Yes. Every call starts by saying it's your business's virtual assistant, and says so if the call is recorded.

How many properties can ATLAS run?

There's no cap. Pillar covers up to 5 properties, Titan up to 15 and Celestial up to 40, and you can add as many extra properties as you need on any plan. Very large portfolios get a tailored setup.

What does ATLAS stand for?

Adaptive TurnKey Logistics and Automation System. It handles the logistics of your short-stay business and automates the rest.

Do I have to switch on autopilot straight away?

No. ATLAS runs in draft mode first. You switch on overnight or 24/7 autopilot property by property, once each property's details are complete.
