FAQs

Questions, answered

Can't see yours? Email kyle@turnkeybnbs.com.au.

Where are your stays?

On the Gold Coast and in Brisbane. See every available property at turnkeystays.com.au.

Can I bring my pet?

Most of our properties are pet friendly. Each listing shows its own pet rules, so check the listing before you book.

How do I book?

Pick a property and your dates at turnkeystays.com.au and book online. You can also find some of our properties on Airbnb and Booking.com.

What if I need to cancel?

Bookings made through Airbnb or Booking.com follow the cancellation policy on that listing. Direct bookings are non-refundable once confirmed. See our Refund policy.

Who do I contact during my stay?

The TurnKey team. Email kyle@turnkeybnbs.com.au or call 0436 322 508, 7am to 7pm.
